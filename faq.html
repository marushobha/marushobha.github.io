Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?+
Once your order is shipped via Delhivery, you will receive a tracking link via email and WhatsApp to follow your package live.
What are the shipping charges?+
We offer standard shipping across India. Exact delivery rates are calculated at checkout based on your delivery pincode.
What is your return and exchange policy?+
Items can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.
How do I care for handwoven garments?+
We recommend gentle hand washing separately in cold water with mild detergent. Do not soak or bleach.
