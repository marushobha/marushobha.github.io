Customer Policies

Before launch: confirm that checkout, shipping and refund processes match these policies. These policy drafts do not replace legal advice.

Return, Exchange & Refund Policy

Marushobha aims to provide accurate product information and quality clothing. Please inspect your order after delivery.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery at marushobha.jj@gmail.com or WhatsApp 8700944947. Include your order details and, where reasonably available, photographs of the item and packaging. An unboxing video may help but its absence alone will not automatically invalidate a claim. For a verified damaged, defective or incorrect item, Marushobha will arrange or bear reasonable return shipping costs.

Size exchanges

Size exchanges may be requested subject to availability. Items should be unused, unwashed, unaltered and returned with original tags and packaging. Contact us before sending an item back so we can provide instructions.

Change-of-mind returns

We generally do not accept returns for change of mind or ordering the wrong size. This does not limit any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

Refunds

If an approved exchange cannot be fulfilled because the requested size is unavailable, we will discuss the applicable refund process with you. Where legally permitted and agreed, refunds may be sent to bank-account details supplied by the customer during the return process. Once a refund is approved and processed, the amount will generally be credited within 7–10 business days. Actual credit time may vary by bank, payment provider and refund method. If the amount is not credited within this period, contact us with your order number and refund reference. This timeline does not override any shorter period required by applicable law or payment-provider requirements.

Shipping Policy

Orders are intended to be processed within 2–3 business days after payment confirmation. Estimated delivery is generally 5–8 business days after dispatch for serviceable Indian locations; remote areas, courier delays, weather and other events may affect delivery.

Delhivery is the intended shipping partner, subject to serviceability and confirmation of the live fulfilment arrangement. Shipping charges and delivery estimates must be shown before checkout is enabled. Do not rely on a live Delhivery rate unless the integration is active and tested.

Please provide a complete address, PIN code and reachable phone number. Tracking details will be shared when available. If a parcel arrives damaged or contains the wrong item, contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We will review the issue and advise the next steps.

Product descriptions, fabric composition, available sizes, prices and delivery estimates should be reviewed carefully before ordering.

Privacy Policy

MARUSHOBHA should collect only the personal information needed to operate the website, respond to enquiries, manage accounts and orders, arrange delivery, process payments when enabled, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Depending on the features actually enabled, information may include name, email, phone number, delivery address, account/authentication details, order information, customer messages and limited technical data. Firebase/Google authentication, a payment provider, a courier such as Delhivery or analytics tools should be named as data processors/recipients only when they are actually used.

Information should be accessed only by authorised people, protected with appropriate safeguards, retained only as needed for the stated purposes and legal obligations, and not sold to advertisers. Customers may contact us to ask about their personal information or raise a privacy concern.

Privacy contact: marushobha.jj@gmail.com. This draft must be checked against the website's actual Firebase, form, analytics, payment and shipping configuration and updated for applicable Indian data-protection requirements before launch.

Payment Policy

Marushobha intends to accept prepaid orders only; Cash on Delivery is not available. Online payment activation is currently in progress. Do not place or accept live orders until an active payment method is displayed at checkout and successful, failed, cancelled and refunded transactions have been tested.

When online payments are enabled, the payment options, total amount, applicable shipping charges and any other charges should be displayed before the customer confirms payment. An order must not be marked paid until payment confirmation is received and verified. Never send card PINs, passwords or OTPs to Marushobha by email or WhatsApp.

Approved refunds will generally be credited within 7–10 business days after processing. Actual credit time may vary by bank, payment provider and refund method. If the amount is not credited within this period, contact us with your order number and refund reference. This timeline does not override any shorter period required by applicable law or payment-provider requirements. For payment questions, contact marushobha.jj@gmail.com or WhatsApp 8700944947.
