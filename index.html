Our Story

Marushobha was born from a simple thought: fashion should feel modern, but never disconnected from its roots. India has an extraordinary textile heritage—Khadi, Kota Doria, Bandhani, and Leheriya carrying stories of generations of craftsmanship.

We transform this heritage through contemporary silhouettes, comfortable construction, and effortless styling. For the woman who desires comfort without sacrificing elegance, and seeks personal style beyond fast fleeting trends.
