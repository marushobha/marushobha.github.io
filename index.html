Signature Design Concept

Kota Doria × Contemporary Denim

Traditional lightness meets contemporary attitude. Kota Doria is prized for its signature square-check weave (khat), rendering it weightless, airy, and translucent.

By counterbalancing this artisanal delicacy with crisp, structured modern denim, Marushobha establishes a completely fresh design language: effortlessly casual yet deeply refined.

Textile Lineage

Fabrics We Celebrate

We look at fabrics not as raw materials, but as cultural archives. Here are the textiles at the foundation of our silhouettes.

Weightless Grace

Kota Doria

Distinctive square checks called khats created through a delicate intertwining of fine cotton and silk threads. Imparts an airy, translucent drape without bulk.

Origin: Kota, Rajasthan Handloom
Simplicity & Character

Handspun Khadi

Textured, breathe-easy handspun yarn that softens with each wear. We explore Khadi through sharp collars, architectural lines, and tailored coordinates.

Origin: Artisanal Clusters Handspun
Rhythm & Color

Lehariya & Bandhani

Wave patterns and resist-dyed dots bringing centuries of celebratory flair. We temper their vibrant rhythm with minimalist cuts and neutral denim layers.

Origin: Western India Resist Dye
Our Motive & Belief

“Fashion can be modern without losing its roots.”

Marushobha was born from a simple question: Why should traditional Indian fabrics be limited to traditional clothing?

We believe fabrics with centuries of heritage deserve a place in the contemporary everyday wardrobe. Our clothing is designed for the woman who values individuality, subtle luxury, and garments with their own voice.
